Prof. Eitan Friedman, MD, PhD

Medical geneticist · Internist · Founding faculty

Four decades in medicine, cancer genetics and medical education. Prof. Friedman founded the Oncogenetics Unit at Sheba Medical Center and directed it from 1995 to 2021, established the Meirav High-Risk Clinic for women at inherited risk of breast and ovarian cancer, and today directs the oncogenetics service at Assuta Medical Center in Tel Aviv.

His research — much of it on BRCA1 and BRCA2 — has centered on translating inherited cancer risk into practical prevention, screening and early detection. Alongside it he has taught physicians and the public for years, through university teaching, professional courses, children's books and television.

Good medicine should not only treat disease. It should help people understand their risks, prevent disease whenever possible, and make reliable medical knowledge clear, practical and accessible.

Localization

One curriculum.
Every language.

The same clinically reviewed lesson can be delivered in the language your clinicians actually think in — without reshooting, rescheduling or waiting on a production calendar.

The science is human

Written and reviewed by Prof. Friedman

Every lesson is scoped, written and scientifically approved by the faculty. Nothing reaches a clinician that a physician has not signed off on — the medical judgment is not automated, and never will be.

The delivery is synthetic

Produced with an AI presenter

Lessons are presented by a synthetic presenter built with the faculty's consent and likeness. We say so openly: in medical education, how knowledge is produced is part of whether it can be trusted.

The result is reach

New languages in weeks, not seasons

Adapting a track to a new language becomes an editorial task rather than a production one — which is what makes a national or institution-wide rollout realistic.

Language adaptation includes medical review by a clinician who works in that language. A translated lesson that is not clinically checked is not a Med5 lesson.
