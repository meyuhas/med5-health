Genetics education for physicians

Genetics matters in every specialty.
Learn it in five minutes.

Genetic testing now touches almost every field of medicine, and most physicians ordering it were never trained as geneticists. Med5 closes that gap with short, focused modules: when genetics matters, which test to consider, and what the result means for care.

Not training geneticists

The goal is practicing clinicians who recognize when genetics is relevant — not a second specialty. Every module is scoped to what a physician actually needs to retain and use.

Microlearning, by design

Concepts are split into short mini-lectures, each answering one defined clinical or scientific question. Built for physicians between patients, not for a lecture hall.

Foundations to clinical action

From DNA, RNA and the central dogma through to hereditary cancer syndromes, BRCA1/2, risk assessment and the therapeutic implications of a genetic finding.

Adaptable to a health system

Content can be localized by language, physician specialty, local testing pathways and population-specific genetic considerations — adaptation, not just translation.

Curriculum

The educational library

Learn what is clinically relevant, understand why it matters, and know when to use genetics in practice.

  • Basic genetics & molecular biology

    DNA, RNA and the central dogma — the vocabulary the rest of the library depends on.

  • Oncogenetics

    Hereditary cancer syndromes, BRCA1/2, risk assessment and therapeutic implications.

  • Prenatal & reproductive genetics

    Carrier screening, prenatal testing pathways and how results shape counseling.

  • Cardiogenetics

    Inherited cardiac conditions and when a cardiology workup becomes a genetics question.

  • Neurogenetics

    Genetic contributions to neurological disease and their diagnostic pathways.

  • Genomic medicine

    Additional clinically relevant areas as genomic testing reaches further into practice.

Scientific leadership

Prof. Eitan Friedman, MD, PhD

Scientific content is led by Prof. Eitan Friedman, an internationally recognized physician and researcher in genetics, internal medicine, molecular biology and hereditary cancer genetics.

The platform is built on a physician-to-physician philosophy: complex genetic concepts translated into concise, clinically meaningful information. The short format demands scientific prioritization — deciding not only what is accurate, but what a practicing physician actually needs to carry into the clinic.

For laboratories & health systems

Education as the bridge between the lab and the clinic

Access to genetic testing is no longer the bottleneck. Who to test, which test, what the result means, and how it changes care — that is where education pays off. We work with partners in structured phases.

  1. 01

    Needs assessment

    Define the target physicians, clinical priorities, language requirements and the real educational gaps.

  2. 02

    Pilot program

    A selected curriculum for a defined physician group, in the clinical area where genetics is most relevant locally.

  3. 03

    Evaluation

    Assess engagement, physician feedback and educational value — and what needs localization.

  4. 04

    Localization & expansion

    Adapt and extend the curriculum to local clinical needs, then broaden implementation together.
